Some Things To Think About

1
Run-rate basis. The $31.6M annualizes all 305 seats at 40 hrs/wk for a full year — 2.3x the $13.5M 2025 actual, which reflected ramp, turnover, and partial assignments. State the basis explicitly for Finance.
2
RPh On the Go is the largest vendor and sits outside the RFP. $8.87M and 65 seats today vs. $0.31M in 2025 actuals. Its savings line assumes benchmark renegotiation on a separate track — clinical/pharmacy staffing was not bid. Recommend adding it to the vendor strategy explicitly.
3
1099 migration is a compliance investment, not savings. N/A (4) + Independent (5) reconcile exactly to the $1.586M direct-1099 line. Moving them to agency W-2 or AOR adds a 10–20% platform fee — roughly $160K–$320K — per the risk framework. Footnote it separately.
4
IntelliPro upside is pilot-contingent. The 26.8% fill rate is a global blended figure, not AssistRx-specific, and no SOC 2 yet. Position the $740K as contingent on a successful pilot, not committed.
5
CitiusTech watch-out. 75% of attrition cases were performance terminations in key delivery pods — rework risk could erode consolidation savings. Negotiate replacement guarantees accordingly.
6
Soft savings not booked above. Reallocating requisitions from ODG (53% fill) toward CitiusTech (87%) and Neutrino (83%) cuts vacancy days, and adding a replacement guarantee where ODG has none avoids bad-start rework. Real, but qualitative.
7
Do not add the $8.94M to the RFP scenarios. Switch Agency and Renegotiate Rate overlap the compression and rebate levers above, and FTE conversion assumes markup elimination net of loaded benefits, before any conversion fees owed under current agreements. Present the two views side by side, with seat-level validation by Finance before committing a combined number.
