What this means

Capacity has outrun demand

Placements fell 71% from Q1 to Q3 while the team stayed the same size, and 741 reqs sit on hold. Internal moves went from 1 a quarter to 115. The Q4 question is redeployment, not headcount.

Speed is not the problem

Median time to fill held at 28 days all year (Q1 26, Q2 31, Q3 28). What slipped is hitting the agreed target date — 92% in Q1 down to 73% in Q3, with May at 61% and June at 50%.

Retention is flat, not improving

Q3's 85% headline is survivorship. On the comparable 90-day measure Q1 was 80.6% and Q2 79.2%. Contractor recruiters sit at 55.4% against 73.9% for the core team.

Half the exits are show-up failures

140 of 294 exits were attendance, job abandonment or no-shows. That sits upstream in screening and pre-start contact, not in management.

The data needs one fix

152 hires cannot be confirmed against HR records, 172 target-fill dates carry year typos, and 79% of September rows have no REQ number. Putting the ADP Associate ID on the tracker at offer-accept closes most of it.

Rehires underperform

67.7% retention on 31 rehires, well below external hires at 71.2%. Worth a harder look at the rehire approval bar.
