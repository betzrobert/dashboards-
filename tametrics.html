Estimated Cost of Roles Filled

Estimated $ = roles filled in 2026 multiplied by the comp band midpoint for that job title, with hourly bands annualized at 2,080 hours. Roles whose job title had no exact match in the comp bands file are excluded from the dollar figure, so treat every number here as a floor.
