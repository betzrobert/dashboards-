Measurement window
Two data sources, labeled throughout.
• Trend (Waves 4–6): per-wave favorability carried from the previously cell-verified wave-level exports. Within the June 1 – October 1, 2026 window, three refreshes closed — Wave 4 (Jun ’26), Wave 5 (Jun ’26) and Wave 6 (closed Jul 20). No survey wave closed between Jul 20 and Oct 1, so these three are the full set for this window.
• Pooled snapshot: the three uploaded All Results files are single aggregates spanning 01 Nov 2025–05 Oct 2026 with no date or wave column, so they can neither be clipped to the June–October window nor produce a trend — they supply the current all-enrollee averages shown in the snapshot tables and the 90-day manager view.
To rebuild the trend purely from source, a dated per-wave Culture Amp export (or a comparison export segmented by survey-close date) is needed.

60-Day cohort

The 60-day cohort is the clearest good-news story in the window. On the largest 60-day sample to date (n=90), Overall rebounded +11.0 pts to a new high, and Communication recovered from 70.1% to 82.6% — reversing the Wave 5 dip that earlier decks flagged as a watch-item.

90-Day cohort

The headline reversal is here: the 90-day “no quality 1:1” gap fell from 73% to 32% on the largest 90-day cohort measured (n=165) — the biggest single-wave improvement anywhere in the dataset, after five waves of the gap worsening. Overall favorability stepped down to 82.3%, but that is the cohort-size effect described on the Overview, not a deterioration.
