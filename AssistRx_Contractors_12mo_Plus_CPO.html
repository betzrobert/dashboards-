What we are asking

Recommended decisions

Immediate

No approval needed beyond the owning leader
  1. Decide the 9 seats past seven years — convert, extend with a documented business case, or end. Median 94 months on assignment.
  2. Brief Mettler, Lynn — 86% of the cohort sits in that org, so one conversation covers most of it.
  3. Backfill titles on the 89 generic records so conversion can be priced against a real job level.
  4. Confirm the 3 direct 1099s and 2 per diem seats in this cohort — long tenure plus no agency is the highest classification risk combination.

Standing control

So this population does not rebuild
  1. Convert-or-end gate at 12 months — no assignment renews past 12 months without a documented decision by the owning manager.
  2. Require assignment end dates — the current record has start dates only, which is why tenure accumulated silently.
  3. Monthly tenure report by manager — surface seats at 9 months so the decision is made before the anniversary.
  4. Conversion-fee waivers in the new MSAs — the vendor RFP is live now, which makes this the moment to remove the cost of converting.
