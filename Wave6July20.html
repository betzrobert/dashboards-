30-Day Trends
▲ 30-Day Overall surged to 92.0% in Wave 4 — new all-time high (+8.1 pts vs Wave 1)

After dipping to 78.8% in Wave 2, the 30-day experience fully recovered and now exceeds any prior result. "Made the right decision" hit 94%, "Would recommend" 92%.

▲ 30-Day Onboarding Experience: 84.4% → 81.7% → 81.1% → 90.3%

After two refreshes of decline, a 9.2-point surge in Wave 4. "Accurate info during recruitment" jumped from 66% to 82%.

▲ 30-Day Team & Culture: 85.7% → 80.6% → 81.6% → 91.3% — New high

"Team members and leaders supportive" hit 94% in Wave 4. All three sub-items exceed 88%.

▲ Training item recovering: 65% → 59% → 67% → 76%

"Efficient training on procedures" rose 17 points from its Wave 2 low.

▲ 30-Day Overall reaches its highest point yet: 83.9% → 78.8% → 83.5% → 92.0% → 96.2% → 97.4%

Fourth consecutive increase — now the strongest 30-day wave on record. Note Wave 6 is a small early cohort (n=13), so the reading is directional; it confirms the sustained upward trajectory rather than establishing a large-sample high.

▲ 30-Day Onboarding Experience keeps climbing: 81.1% → 90.3% → 94.3% → 98.1%

Three consecutive jumps to a new high. "Accurate info during recruitment," "supported in onboarding," and "provided benefits information" all reached 100% favorable in Wave 6 (n=13).

60-Day Trends
▼ 60-Day Onboarding Experience: 78.9% → 75.6% → 75.2% → 71.7% — Declined every refresh

"Role expectations communicated through onboarding" stuck at 63–66% across all waves. Has not responded to any intervention so far.

▲ 60-Day Overall recovers to a new high: 82.5% → 83.2% → 81.9% → 78.1% → 78.4% → 89.4%

After the Wave 4–5 dip, an 11.0-point rebound in Wave 6 (n=90) — the strongest 60-day Overall on record. Belonging and job-satisfaction sub-items recovered in tandem across the larger cohort.

▼ 60-Day Team & Culture: 88.3% → 87.8% → 87.2% → 83.3% — Slow consistent erosion

5 points lower than Wave 1. "Felt welcomed since starting" dropped from 90% to 83%.

● "Discussed development goals" at 60-Day, still the stubborn item: 52% → 48% → 51% → 52.8% → 47.5% → ~63%

Wave 6 shows the first meaningful lift on the larger cohort, but development-goal conversations remain the softest sub-item within an otherwise strong 60-day picture. Worth continued attention even as the rest of the factor recovers.

▲ 60-Day Communication rebounds strongly: 77.6% → 75.1% → 70.1% → 82.6% — new high, reversing the Wave 5 dip

A 12.5-point single-wave recovery on the largest 60-day cohort to date (n=90). The Wave 5 low now reads as a small-sample trough rather than a sustained decline — Communication is at its best level across all six waves.

▲ 60-Day Onboarding Experience jumps off the floor: 75.2% → 71.7% → 71.6% → 86.7% — new high

After four flat-to-declining waves, a 15.1-point surge in Wave 6 (n=90). The factor that had been the 60-day dataset floor is now among its strongest — the single largest wave-over-wave move in the 60-day series.

90-Day Trends (Waves 1, 3, 4, 5, 6)
● 90-Day Overall steps down on the largest cohort: 81.9% → 85.5% → 84.0% → 89.1% → 82.3%

Wave 6 (n=165) is more than 3× the size of Wave 5 (n=46), so this is the most representative 90-day read to date. The step down from the small-sample Wave 5 peak largely reflects that shift to a fuller cohort; 82.3% is squarely in line with the Wave 1–4 range.

▲ 90-Day Job Satisfaction holds steady: 83.8% → 88.4% → 85.6% → 83.3% → 82.0%

By 90 days employees remain consistently clear on their roles and find meaning in their work. Stable across the larger Wave 6 cohort.

● "Onboarding prepared me for my role" recovers: 66% → 76% → 71.5% → 65.2% → 74.2%

Wave 6 (n=165) reverses the two-wave slide and returns this item near its Wave 3 level. Perceived role-readiness at 90 days is back in a healthy range on the largest cohort measured, though still a notch below the strongest factors.

▲ MAJOR REVERSAL: 90-Day "1:1 with direct leadership" gap drops sharply: 57% → 60% → 58% → 64% → 73% → 32%

The dataset's most persistent negative trend reverses hard in Wave 6. On the largest 90-day cohort measured (n=165), the share reporting no quality 1:1 time falls from nearly 3 in 4 to under 1 in 3 — the biggest single-wave improvement anywhere in the data. Worth confirming the gain holds as future waves land, but a clear and encouraging break in the trend.

▲ "Discussed development goals" at 90-Day improves: 54% → 54% → 57% → 64.4% in Wave 6

Now separately reported again, this item reaches its highest level in the 90-day series. Together with the sharp drop in the 1:1 gap, Wave 6 points to a genuine step forward in manager-coaching behaviors at the 90-day mark — a reversal of the prior systemic concern.

Among the strongest large-cohort profiles this wave — a model for the manager onboarding playbook.

Overall 100%. The clearest 60-Day coaching priority this wave.
