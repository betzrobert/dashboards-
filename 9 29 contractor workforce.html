About this snapshot and its measures

Source: 9 29 Contractor Workforce.xlsx, Sheet1. Includes the 290 named records where HC = 1; each has an Active position status and a unique associate ID. The blank row and applied-filter note are excluded. The workbook’s filter note says “Headcount is 1” and “Date is on or after 1/1/26”; the date field used by that filter is not identified. The filename specifies 9/29 without a year.

All counts, percentages and charts reflect the current filters. Offshore means the location description is exactly “Offshore”; other locations are not automatically classified as domestic. Tenure bands and hire / rehire dates are taken directly from the source. Missing locations appear as “Not specified.” The “New Hire Ct” field is not presented as a period metric because its reporting window is not defined.

This is a fixed workforce snapshot. Supplier names, spend, contract end dates and trend history are not supplied. Brand styling follows the AssistRx Style Guide, May 2025: dark teal, light teal, dark blue, neutral grays and Arial for digital usage.
