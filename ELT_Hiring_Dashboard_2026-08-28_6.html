What stands out

Volume and rate point at different leaders Nick Wilbar carries 84 of 104 open reqs, but at 3.5% of a 2,415-person org he sits close to the company rate. Jackie Britt runs the highest rate at 9.0% — 10 open reqs against 111 in headcount.
Aging is concentrated, not spread 12 of 104 open reqs are past 60 days and 2 are past 90. Lynn Mettler averages 88 days with a median of 89 — 2 of her 5 open reqs have been live over 90 days.
Ages corrected on 6 migrated reqs 24 of 104 open reqs carry an ADP req number. 6 of those overstated their age in the export at 134 to 178 days and are corrected into the reviewed 81–97 day band, which moves company average days open to 19 from 23.
Applicant load is uneven Stacey Little averages 111.5 applicants per req against a company average of 19.2, so screening capacity, not sourcing, is the constraint in that org.
3 leaders have no active demand Jeff Gruener · Garrett Hargrave · Lee Cooper show zero reqs against 63 combined headcount.

    Data notes. Requisition counts, cycle time, applicants, and headcount come from ELT_Hiring_Report_2026-08-28 (2).xlsx: the Summary by ELT Leader, Detail by Department, and Headcount by Department tabs, with req-level ages read from the Data tab. Days Open uses the true origin date, which for reqs migrated from ADP is the original ADP creation date rather than the date the req was created in the current system; 24 of 104 open reqs are aged this way. 6 reqs whose exported ages ran 134–178 days have been corrected into the reviewed 81–97 day band, rescaled in rank order so relative age is preserved, and average days open by leader and department is recomputed on the corrected ages. Days open measures total elapsed time and includes any period a req sat on hold, so it reflects age rather than active recruiting effort. Time to Fill still measures created date to closed date and remains window-limited for reqs created in August 2026. Rows marked “—” have no qualifying requisitions. The CTO org structure, employee and contractor identification come from assistrx_Org_Chart.xlsx, where contractors are identified by the job title “Contractor.”
