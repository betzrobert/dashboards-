What the numbers say

Two reads for leadership

HUB carries the volume, not the aging

HUB holds 69% of open reqs but averages 20.0 days open — a third faster than the enterprise average. Volume is concentrated where the pipeline is moving.

Non-HUB is growth, HUB is replacement

Non-HUB runs 65% new headcount; HUB runs 57% backfill. Corporate and Technology demand is expansion-driven, while HUB demand is largely attrition replacement.
