Callouts

Ten things this data says
1
The program more than doubled without a strategy. The $32.6M run rate is 2.4x the $13.5M in 2025 actuals.
2
310 seats at $105K each is 10% of the workforce. A workforce-strategy decision, not a procurement errand.
3
The largest vendor was never bid. RPh On the Go: $8.87M, 27% of spend — needs its own negotiation track.
4
54% of spend sits in two vendors. RPh On the Go + ODG hold $17.5M combined.
5
ODG’s negotiating window is now. 53% fill, no replacement guarantee, four competitors in the room the same week.
6
60% of spend is already at the table. $19.6M sits with the four incumbent RFP finalists.
7
The RFP is worth ~$1.38M a year, base case. Scaling from ~$800K conservative to ~$2.93M aggressive.
8
The outside-RFP opportunity is ~7x the base case. $9.4M identified, with FTE conversion carrying $7.25M.
9
Conversion-fee waivers may be the most valuable MSA term. They unlock the $7.25M FTE block affordably.
10
$713K needs no negotiation at all. Six Term/Reduce HC seats are 100% savings, actionable this quarter.
