What the numbers say

Contractor recruiters retain 18 points worse than core

55.4% vs 73.2%. 39% of contractor hires are gone inside 90 days versus 19% for core — and the gap holds on the 90-day measure, so it is not a timing artifact.

81 exits were no-shows or job abandonment

28% of all terminations. These are hires who never really engaged — the cheapest retention win available, and it sits upstream in screening and pre-start contact, not in management.

Rehires underperform badly

67.7% retention on 31 rehires, roughly 13 points below external hires. Worth a harder look at the rehire approval bar.

Advanced Academy Assoc is the weakest role

48.4% on 64 hires. The Academy pipeline loses about half its intake — either the screen or the program itself needs attention.

Arizona, Tempe is the weakest site

58.1% on 93 hires, roughly 15 points below Southpark Orlando. A newer site with a thinner local labor pool is the likely driver.

Spread across core recruiters is real but modest

Sabrina leads at 87.5% and Noelle trails at 47.5% among recruiters with 20+ confirmed hires. Most of that gap tracks the role and site mix each recruiter was assigned rather than recruiter quality — compare within role before drawing conclusions.

Two more things to clean up in the tracker

11 people appear on more than one hire row

23 rows in total, with different start dates, so they survive duplicate removal and are counted twice in the hire denominator. It affects every recruiter's numerator and denominator the same way and does not move retention materially, but it does overstate gross hire volume by roughly 12 hires.

Hire Type is entered 39 different ways

"Referral", "Refferal", "Referal", "External - Referral", "Rehire - HR approved 3/23" and so on. The source cuts above normalise these into External / Referral / Rehire, but a dropdown on the tracker would make this reliable without cleanup.
